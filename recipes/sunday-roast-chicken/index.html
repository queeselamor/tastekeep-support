  • 1 hr 45 min
  • 4 servings

The bag keeps the steam in, so the meat stays juicy. Cut it open for the last 15 minutes and the skin turns golden.

Ingredients

  • whole chicken1, about 1.6 kg
  • soft butter40 g
  • garlic, crushed4 cloves
  • thyme4 sprigs
  • lemon, halved1
  • apple, quartered1
  • salt and pepper
  • roasting bag1

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C / 400°F. Mix the butter with the garlic, thyme leaves, salt and pepper and rub it all over the chicken. Put the lemon and apple inside, then tie the chicken in a roasting bag and set it in a roasting dish.
  2. Roast for 1 hour 15 minutes.
  3. Cut the bag open and roast for 15 minutes more, until the skin is golden. Rest for 10 minutes before carving.
