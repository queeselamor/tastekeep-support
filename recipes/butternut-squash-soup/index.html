  • 55 min
  • 4 servings
  • Soup

Squash and cherry tomatoes roast on one tray, then go into the blender with stock and cream. The chili and radish on top are the crunch.

Ingredients

  • butternut squash1, about 1.2 kg
  • cherry tomatoes250 g
  • onion1
  • garlic3 cloves
  • olive oil2 tbsp
  • hot vegetable stock750 ml
  • cream100 ml
  • salt and pepper
  • red chili, radishes and parsley, to serve

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C / 400°F. Halve the squash and scoop out the seeds.
  2. Halve the tomatoes and cut the onion into wedges.
  3. Lay everything on a baking tray, cut side up, with the garlic. Drizzle with the oil and season.
  4. Roast for 40 minutes, until the squash is soft.
  5. Scoop the squash out of its skin and blend it with the tomatoes, onion, garlic, stock and cream until smooth. Serve with sliced chili, radish and parsley.
