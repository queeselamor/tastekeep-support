A moist loaf for very ripe bananas. One bowl, about an hour, 10 slices.

Ingredients

  • very ripe bananas3
  • melted butter100 g
  • brown sugar150 g
  • eggs2
  • plain flour250 g
  • baking soda1 tsp

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 175°C / 350°F and line a loaf tin.
  2. Mash the bananas and whisk in the butter, sugar and eggs.
  3. Fold in the flour and baking soda.
  4. Bake for 55 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean. Cool for 10 minutes before slicing.
