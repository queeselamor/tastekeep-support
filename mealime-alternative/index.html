Switching from Mealime

Mealime is gone. Here is a recipe book that stays.

Mealime shut down on October 21, 2026, and it never had an export. If you planned your weeks in it, this page is how to rebuild your recipe box in an evening, in an app you buy once and keep.

Free on the App Store.
No account, no subscription.

What you get back

  • A weekly planner from your own recipes. Breakfast to dinner for the days ahead, picked from your book. Save a week you liked as a template and bring it back.
  • One shopping list, sorted by aisle. Built from the week or from a single recipe, quantities added up, shareable with whoever goes to the store.
  • Cooking mode with timers. One step on screen in large type, a timer per step that stays on the Lock Screen while your hands are busy.
  • Import from any link or note. Paste a link to a recipe page, or the text of a note, and TasteKeep fills in the title, photo, ingredients and steps.

Moving your recipes

  1. Write down the dinners you cooked most from Mealime. Most people have eight to twelve.
  2. For each one, find a version you like on any recipe site, copy the link, open TasteKeep and tap Import. Check the recipe and save it. In Safari, tap Share and choose TasteKeep instead.
  3. Recipes you know by heart: type them as a note, paste the text into Import, and they become recipes with ingredients and steps.
  4. Open the planner, add the week, build the list. Sunday evening is enough for a week.

What it costs

The recipe book, import, cooking mode and one shopping list are free. The weekly planner, week templates and lists built from plans are TasteKeep Pro, a one-time purchase. There is nothing to cancel later.

What happens if this app shuts down too

Your recipes stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. There is no account and no server holding your data, and any recipe exports as a PDF or as text whenever you want. The App Store label for TasteKeep reads “Data Not Collected”.
