TasteKeep

Paste a link, get the recipe

Found a recipe online? Paste the link into TasteKeep, and it fills in the title, photo, ingredients and steps. From Safari, tap Share and choose TasteKeep. Check the recipe, save it, cook from it with step-by-step mode and Lock Screen timers. Free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. No account, no subscription.
